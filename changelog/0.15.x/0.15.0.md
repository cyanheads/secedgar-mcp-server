# 0.15.0 — 2026-07-27

## Added

- **`secedgar_get_beneficial_owners`** — 5%-and-over blockholders of an issuer, parsed from the structured SCHEDULE 13D / SCHEDULE 13G XML SEC has required since 2024-12-18. Filters by `form_kind` (13D activist vs. 13G passive) and `include_amendments`; returns each reporting person separately (voting/dispositive power and percent of class are per person, never summed across a joint filing) and reports `legacy_filings_before_coverage` for pre-2024-12-18 text filings the schema can't parse. Full parsed set registers as `df_<id>`, one row per reporting person. New service `src/services/edgar/beneficial-ownership-parser.ts` branches per schema (13D repeats `reportingPersons/reportingPersonInfo`, 13G repeats `coverPageHeaderReportingPersonDetails` with the powers nested and the percentage field named `classPercent`). ([#83](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/83))
- **`secedgar_get_fund_holdings`** — ETF and mutual fund portfolio holdings from the quarterly NPORT-P report. Resolves a fund ticker, series ID, or registrant CIK to the matching series-scoped filing via EDGAR's company-browse Atom feed (`EdgarApiService.getFundSeriesFilings`, new `resolveFundSeries`/`listFundSeries` backed by a new `bySeriesId` index on the ticker cache); a registrant with more than one listed series throws `series_required` naming the candidates. Positions page largest-first by percent of net assets (`limit`/`offset`); the full report (up to thousands of positions) registers as `df_<id>`. Every result carries `report_period_date` and `publication_lag_days` since NPORT-P publishes on a ~60-day lag. New service `src/services/edgar/nport-parser.ts`. ([#80](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/80))
- **`secedgar://filing-types`** — added an `NPORT-P` row.
- **`secedgar_company_analysis`** — a `blockholder`/`activist`/`beneficial`/`13d`/`13g`/`stake` focus area now routes to a `secedgar_get_beneficial_owners` step alongside the existing insider/institutional ones.
- **`EdgarApiService.tryGetFilingDocumentHead`** — reads a filing document's leading bytes and cancels the stream once a stop marker appears or a byte ceiling is hit, since SEC's archive host ignores `Range` requests; backs the fund-series header scan.
- **`src/services/edgar/xml-nodes.ts`** — prefix-insensitive tag lookup (`findTag`/`findTags`/`childText`/`childTexts`/`childAttr`) and value coercion (`parseNumber`/`toIsoDate`) shared by both new XML parsers.

## Changed

- **`secedgar_search_filings`** — pre-2001 entity-scoped free text (`ticker:`/`cik:` with search terms and a date range before 2001-01-01) is now served instead of rejected: the form+date pre-filter selects candidates, up to 50 documents (`DOCUMENT_SCAN_CAP`) are fetched and matched locally against the query (phrases, `OR`, `-exclusion`, `wildcard*`), and the response's new `scan` field reports `candidates`/`scanned`/`matched`/`capped`. A date range straddling 2001-01-01 now auto-splits at the boundary and merges the EFTS (≥2001) and archive (<2001) sides by date, each row keeping its `source` tag; `total`/`total_is_exact` reflect both sides and a new offset/window-gap notice explains the merged list's shape. Replaces the `straddling_date_range` and `pre2001_full_text_scoped` error reasons — `pre2001_full_text_unscoped` (unscoped pre-2001 free text, still unservable) now covers what those two used to reject. ([#87](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/87))
