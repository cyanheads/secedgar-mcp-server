# 0.15.4 — 2026-09-09

## Fixed

- **`detectHeadings`'s all-caps arm now matches horizontal whitespace only**, so a run of consecutive all-caps lines (a "TABLE OF CONTENTS" header directly above "PART I") no longer fuses into one composite entry ([#105](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/105)).
- **A bare `Item N` marker with its title on a following line is now detected and joined to it** — the shape every Item takes in a 20-F — deduplicated against its TOC occurrence, with the suffix widened to `16A`–`16K` and the trailing period made optional. On a C3is FY2025 20-F the outline went from 15 entries (zero Items) to 42, covering all 30 Items and 3 Parts ([#105](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/105)).
- **A heading repeated 5+ times at distinct offsets is now treated as a running page header**: a bare marker (`Item 1`, `PART I`) drops every occurrence, and any other repeated heading keeps only its first — at most one entry per running header ([#105](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/105)).
- **`secedgar_get_filing`'s `section` match now folds Unicode whitespace and typographic quotes on both operands**, so a heading copied from the rendered outline resolves whether its NBSP runs or curly quotes were normalized to plain characters or not ([#106](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/106)).
