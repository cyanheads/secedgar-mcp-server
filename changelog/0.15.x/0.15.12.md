# 0.15.12 — 2026-10-14

## Changed

- **`secedgar_compare_companies` annual matrices** hold each company's fiscal year under the calendar-year key SEC frames it as, and take a balance-sheet value only on a fiscal-year end, beside the income lines of the year it closes. An annual call asking only for cover-date concepts (`shares_outstanding`) now fails `no_comparable_data` with a hint naming `period_type: "quarterly"`; it used to succeed, pairing interim snapshots with annual income lines ([#171](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/171)).
- **`secedgar_get_financials` `period_type: "annual"`** on an instant concept returns the values dated on the filer's fiscal-year ends; a cover-date concept still fails `no_period_data`, with a hint naming the cause ([#167](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/167)).
- **`secedgar_get_filing` `section`** matches every detected heading, an exact heading (or its title after the Item/Part marker) first. With no heading match it lands on the document line equal to the value, else starting with it (`Liquidity and Capital Resources`), and the notice says so ([#166](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/166), [#169](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/169)).
- **`secedgar_get_filing` `outline`** past 50 detected headings lists every Item and Part heading first (bare table-of-contents copies of titled ones dropped), then the earliest others; the notice gives the detected count ([#169](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/169)).
- **`secedgar_company_search` `filing_limit`** accepts up to 1000. The inline list holds 50, and a larger value is served at 50 with a notice naming the dataframe or the `secedgar_search_filings` call that reaches the rest ([#168](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/168)).
- **`secedgar_dataframe_query`** rejects engine-setting and SQL-from-string functions (`current_setting`, `query_table`) as `denied_function` and a file named as a table (`FROM 'data.csv'`) as `sql_read_only` (mcp-ts-core 0.13.15).
- **Fund-ticker load warning** carries `endpoint: "company_tickers_mf"` in place of the URL.
- **Package description** reads "via MCP. STDIO or Streamable HTTP." across the plugin manifests, `manifest.json`, `server.json`, and the image label; the image builds on `oven/bun` 1.4.3.

## Fixed

- **`secedgar_get_financials` `no_concept_data`** leads its hint with the taxonomy retry the filer's facts support (`Retry with taxonomy: "ifrs-full"`) ([#164](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/164)).
- **`secedgar_get_filing` current 8-K Item headings** (`Item 5.02`, `Item 9.01(d)`, a code abutting its title) reach the outline and `section` ([#165](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/165)).
- **`secedgar_get_filing` all-caps headings** admit digits, dashes, and apostrophes (`NOTE 10`, `MANAGEMENT’S DISCUSSION`) up to 140 characters; a longer all-caps line is a paragraph, not a heading, and a table-of-contents row that runs Items together is not one heading ([#170](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/170)).
- **`secedgar_get_filing` heading numbers** — a table-of-contents `Page N` cell is stripped, and a number after a single space stays (`PROPOSAL NO. 1`, `PROPOSAL NO. 2` no longer merge) ([#172](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/172)).
