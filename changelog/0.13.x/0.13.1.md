# 0.13.1 — 2026-07-26

## Added

- **`deprecatedTagCaveats(tag, label)`** in `concept-series.ts` — matches SEC's `(Deprecated YYYY-MM-DD)` stamp in the taxonomy label already carried on every companyconcept and companyfacts payload, so the check costs no upstream call. `secedgar_get_financials`, `secedgar_get_snapshot`, and `secedgar_compare_companies` thread it into their `caveats`; the snapshot entry is prefixed with the concept name, the comparison entry with the company and concept. ([#98](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/98))
- **`binary` on `secedgar_get_filing`'s document entries** — `true` for scanned pages, PDF exhibits, packaged archives, and `Financial_Report.xlsx`, absent for readable entries. Derived from the filename extension, plus a header type of `GRAPHIC` for oddly-named entries. ([#96](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/96))
- **`binary_document` declared error** on `secedgar_get_filing` — raised from `resolveFilingArchive` before the document body is fetched, naming the entry's inferred type (`GRAPHIC`, `PDF`, `BINARY`) and the readable entries in the same filing. ([#96](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/96))
- **`ifrs_tags` on `secedgar_search_concepts`** and an `ifrs-full:` line in the `secedgar://concepts` table — the IFRS element set is a different list from `tags`, not a synonym of it, and neither surface showed it. Absent for a concept with no IFRS equivalent. ([#99](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/99))

## Changed

- **`ifrsTags` coverage 3 → 31 of the 33 concepts** in `concept-map.ts` — every income-statement, balance-sheet, cash-flow, and per-share entry now resolves under `taxonomy: "ifrs-full"`. Each element is confirmed present in a live 20-F filer's companyfacts rather than read off the taxonomy. `notes_payable` stays unmapped (IFRS presents borrowings as one caption, with no notes/debt split) and `shares_outstanding` remains `dei`. Order is semantic the same way `tags` is: `accounts_receivable` leads with the trade-only caption over the combined trade-and-other one, `share_repurchases` with the IAS 7 financing outflow over the treasury-only line a share-cancelling filer tags zero, `interest_expense` with the exact `InterestExpense` counterpart over the broader `FinanceCosts`. ([#99](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/99))
- **`MIN_QUARTERS_PER_YEAR` 3 → 2** in `fiscal-periods.ts` — two is also the floor at which a year still carries signal, bounding the absent set at two quarters so a stub year reporting one quarter cannot drag a third in. The recency-then-quorum order is unchanged: the newest 4 years carrying quarterly frames are sliced first, then the per-year quorum applies inside that window. ([#100](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/100))
- **Document catalog sorts readable entries ahead of binary ones** in `secedgar_get_filing`'s error catalogs — the 10-per-category sample was otherwise consumed by scan images on filings that carry hundreds of them, handing back a recovery list of names the caller cannot pass. Stable sort, so order within each half is the filing index's own. Names render as `file.jpg [GRAPHIC, binary]`. ([#96](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/96))
- **`caveats` and `period_type` field descriptions** on `secedgar_get_financials`, `secedgar_get_snapshot`, and `secedgar_compare_companies` — reworded for the two-quarter case and the new staleness entry.

## Fixed

- **`revenue` resolved through a tag retired in 2018 for filers reporting gross of assessed tax** — `RevenueFromContractWithCustomerIncludingAssessedTax` is the ASC 606 election used by food, beverage, and tobacco filers, and it was absent from the tag array, so the walk fell past every current tag to `SalesRevenueGoodsNet` and returned a plausible series that stopped years ago. Added at index 2, behind `Revenues` rather than ahead of it: the two disagree for filers that tag gross sales and net-of-excise sales under separate elements, so promoting it over `Revenues` would swap the definition partway back through the history and print a step change that is a tag switch, not a business fact. `RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax` stays at index 0, so a filer reporting both variants still resolves to the net total. ([#98](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/98))
- **`missingQuarterCaveats` emitted nothing for a filer missing two calendar quarters** — the detector required exactly one absent quarter and at least three reported quarters per counting year, so a filer whose remaining fiscal quarters also span non-calendar durations (holding steady at two frame-tagged quarters a year) tripped neither gate and got no caveat at all. It now reports the full set of consistently-absent quarters, with wording and derivation advice that differ between the one- and two-quarter cases — a single absent quarter is the fiscal-Q4 residual and can be derived as the annual total minus the three reported quarters; two can only be derived together. ([#100](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/100))
- **`secedgar_get_filing` returned binary documents as decoded text** — `filingToExtract` runs `html-to-text` over whatever bytes arrive and never inspects a content type, so passing a `.jpg`, `.pdf`, `.zip`, or `.xlsx` entry as `document` succeeded with mojibake `content` and a plausible `content_total_length`, no error raised. Detection is filename-only by design: the canonical `GRAPHIC` type lives in the submission header, which the archive path fetches after the document, and requiring it would trade today's parallel header/submissions fetch for a sequential one. The catalog marker alone could not close this — `document` is an unconstrained string — so the guard sits in the fetch path as well. ([#96](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/96))
