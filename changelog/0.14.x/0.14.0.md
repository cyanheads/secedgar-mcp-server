# 0.14.0 — 2026-07-26

## Added

- **`secedgar_find_holders`** — the reverse direction of `secedgar_get_institutional_holdings`: an issuer in, the institutional managers reporting a position out, each row chaining back by `filer_cik`. Matches 13F-HR information tables through EFTS by `cusip` (the identifier the table itself carries) or, without one, by the issuer's EDGAR-conformed name as a phrase. `quarter` defaults to the newest reporting quarter whose 45-day deadline has passed; the search window runs from the day after that quarter end through the end of the next one, and rows are kept by their reported `period_ending`, so amendments restating an older quarter drop out and a manager that amended this quarter is kept once at its latest filing. Up to 500 filings are fetched — EFTS ignores `size` and answers every request with 100 hits, so the budget is 5 `from`-paged requests — with `total_filings` carrying the full count and `dataset.truncated` flagging the rest. The list is unranked and the `ordering` enrichment says so: search relevance carries no signal about position size. Declared errors: `issuer_not_found`, `ambiguous_issuer`. ([#81](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/81))
- **`secedgar_get_material_events`** — a company's 8-K history with item codes decoded and filterable through `items`, which neither `secedgar_search_filings` nor `secedgar_company_search` can see. `item_distribution` counts every code across the scanned window *before* the filter, so a zero-hit filter returns the codes that are present rather than a dead end. A date window pages into the submissions archive (capped at 10 pages) to reach 8-K filings older than the ~1000-filing recent window, with the depth disclosed as `history_scanned_through`. The full filtered set registers as `df_<id>`, item codes riding as a comma-separated `item_codes` column. Declared errors: `no_match`, `multiple_matches`. ([#82](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/82))
- **`eight-k-items.ts`** — Form 8-K item decode tables for both numbering regimes, with `decodeEightKItem`, `parseEightKItems`, and `EIGHT_K_ITEM_CODES` backing the `items` input enum. Decoding keys off the code's shape — dotted is the current scheme, a bare integer the one before it — never the filing date, because filers reported pre-changeover events under the old numbers for months after Release 33-8400 took effect on 2004-08-23. A code matching a shape but absent from that regime's table comes back with no label rather than a guess.
- **`newestReportedPeriod(facts, taxonomy)`** in `concept-series.ts` — the newest standard calendar period a filer reports anywhere in the concept catalog, and the reference `secedgar_get_snapshot` and `secedgar_compare_companies` measure each line against. Reads the whole catalog rather than the requested concepts, or a single-concept comparison would measure a series against itself. Skips the `dei` namespace, whose cover-page facts stay current for a registrant that stopped reporting under the taxonomy. ([#102](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/102))
- **`preferredTagIndex(units, selection)`** and the `TagSelection` type (`priority` | `coverage`) — `priority` is the default and the existing behavior. Under `coverage` the tag covering the most standard calendar periods for this filer wins, ties broken by declared order, and the losing tags drop out entirely instead of filling the winner's gaps: alternates disagree where they overlap, so a gap-filled series prints a step that is a tag switch rather than a business fact. ([#101](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/101))
- **`ConceptMapping.ifrsTagSelection`** — opt-in per concept and scoped to `ifrsTags`, since a concept's us-gaap array can be a genuine priority ladder while its IFRS counterparts are alternates. `resolveConceptTarget` returns the resolved `tagSelection` alongside the tags.
- **`cleanDisplayName`** exported from `edgar-api-service.ts` — the EFTS `display_names[0]` ticker/CIK parenthetical strip, moved out of `search-filings.tool.ts` so `secedgar_find_holders` reads the same clean entity name.

## Changed

- **`seriesStalenessCaveats(tag, label, newest, reference)`** replaces `deprecatedTagCaveats(tag, label)` — it keeps the retired-tag case and adds the one a taxonomy label cannot mark: a current tag whose series simply ends, which is what a filer migrating to another element or dropping the disclosure looks like. Fires at 730 days behind the reference, two full fiscal years, clear of the ~500 days a healthy annual-only 20-F filer reaches on its own. At most one caveat per line — the retirement stamp wins when both apply, since it names the concrete cause. ([#102](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/102))
- **`secedgar_get_snapshot` and `secedgar_compare_companies` measure staleness against the filer's own newest reported period**, so a filer that stopped filing altogether — every line equally old — is not flagged concept by concept. `secedgar_get_financials` measures against today, because one companyconcept payload holds no filer-wide period and fetching one would cost an extra upstream call. All three read the full deduped set rather than the period-filtered slice.
- **`resolveFrameSeries` and `seriesFromCompanyFacts` take a `TagSelection`**, defaulting to `priority`. `seriesFromCompanyFacts` now reports the `tag`, `label`, `description`, and `unit` of the tag that won for the filer rather than the first one to answer — under `coverage` those differ, and describing a series by a tag that lost every frame would misname the values.
- **`secedgar://filing-types`** carries the full 8-K item tables for both numbering regimes, the current one grouped by its Form 8-K section headings.
- **Ownership tools cross-link both directions** — `secedgar_get_institutional_holdings`' description, input describe text, `@fileoverview`, and its operating-company `no_filings_found` message, recovery hint, and suggestions now route to `secedgar_find_holders` instead of declaring reverse lookup unsupported. The `secedgar_company_analysis` prompt's Institutional Ownership step chains the two, and its Material Events step targets `secedgar_get_material_events`.
- **Server instructions** name `secedgar_get_material_events` and both directions of the ownership pair.

## Fixed

- **`ifrs-full` `stock_based_compensation` resolved a fringe disclosure** — the concept mapped to one IFRS element, `ExpenseFromSharebasedPaymentTransactionsWithEmployees`, and filers split between it and the IFRS 2.51(a) expense total in both directions. SAP returned CY2020 = EUR 46,000,000, a two-period employee-scheme disclosure, in place of its real 11-period series reaching EUR 1,695,000,000; TSM and HSBC returned no data at all. Both elements are now mapped under `ifrsTagSelection: 'coverage'`, so each filer resolves to the one it maintains — no fixed order is right for both, since Sanofi is the exact inversion of SAP. Coverage stays opt-in and `priority` remains the general rule: ranking `revenue` that way would hand Molson Coors either an element SEC retired in 2018 or its gross-of-excise line in place of the continuous net series. ([#101](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/101))
