# 0.19.1 — 2026-11-13

## Added

- **Former tickers resolve in every company-resolving tool** ([#237](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/237)): `AMRK` reads as Gold.com, Inc., and `resolvedAs` names the holder and the latest monthly capture of SEC's ticker file that lists the symbol. The symbols come from a committed asset, `src/services/edgar/data/former-tickers.json`, written by `bun run gen:former-tickers` from the Wayback Machine's monthly captures of the ticker file.
- **An earlier holder of a listed symbol is named in `resolvedAs`** ([#237](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/237)): `XOM` still resolves to ExxonMobil Holdings Corp and reads that EXXON MOBIL CORP held it before.
- **A word-order name tier** ([#236](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/236)) matches a multi-word query that reorders a name's words when no other name tier matches: `Charles Schwab` finds SCHWAB CHARLES CORP, `JB Hunt Transport Services` finds HUNT J B TRANSPORT SERVICES INC.
- **`secedgar_company_search` looks a missed name up in EDGAR's entity index** ([#236](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/236)), which holds filers with no ticker: `Exxon Mobil` resolves to EXXON MOBIL CORP, with `resolvedAs` saying so. When SEC fails the lookup, the call fails with that failure's reason (`efts_degraded_response` among them) rather than `no_match`.
- **`secedgar_company_search` searches 10-K subsidiaries exhibits (EX-21) when no entity-index hit matches the name** ([#236](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/236)). The filers of a matching exhibit come back in the `no_match` error's `data.parents` (`Vertafore` → ROPER TECHNOLOGIES INC).

## Changed

- **An uppercase former ticker answers ahead of a name the input only begins or contains** ([#237](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/237)), when its holder is still listed under another symbol or the names find no single company. A current name the input matches exactly, or the one company the names find for a holder listed no more, keeps its answer. Answers that change:
  - `BK` resolves to Bank of New York Mellon Corp (CIK 0001390777, listed as BNY), where 0.19.0 answered BK Technologies.
  - `FORD` resolves to Forward Industries, Inc. (FWDI), where 0.19.0 answered Ford Motor Co; `resolvedAs` says so, and `Ford Motor` or `F` still reaches the automaker.
  - `STR` resolves to its most recent holder, Sitio Royalties, instead of failing as ambiguous.
  - `ticker:MRO` in `secedgar_search_filings` scopes to Marathon Oil instead of failing `unresolved_ticker`.
- **`secedgar_get_institutional_holdings`' `ambiguous_entity` candidates carry SEC's ticker, split from the name** ([#236](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/236)).
- **The `resolvedAs` and `effectiveQuery` field descriptions** cover former tickers, earlier holders, and entity-index matches.

## Fixed

- **SEC's HTTP error response body no longer reaches callers in the error's `data`** (`body`, `responseBody`) ([#244](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/244)). It is attached to the error as its cause and written to the server's failure log; `status`, `url`, `retryAfter`, and the recovery hint stay.
- **`secedgar_company_search`'s text leaves out the SIC when SEC records none** ([#236](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/236)), common for a filer found only in EDGAR's entity index, instead of printing `SIC:  ()`.
