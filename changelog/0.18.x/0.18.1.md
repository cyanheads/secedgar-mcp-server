# 0.18.1 — 2026-11-03

## Changed

- **`secedgar_company_search` and `secedgar_get_material_events` fail an ambiguous name as `ambiguous_company`** ([#228](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/228)), a `ValidationError` with the candidates in `data.matches`, as `secedgar_get_financials` does. It replaces `multiple_matches` (`NotFound`); callers branching on that reason switch. `secedgar_company_search` keeps `data.query`.

## Fixed

- **`secedgar_get_filing` body in `content[]`** ([#226](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/226)) sits in a fenced block between the unchanged BEGIN/END sentinels, so a CommonMark parse no longer turns dash rules into breaks, `*` and `1.` lines into lists, or indented lines into code.
- **Ambiguity errors list 10 candidates and carry `data.total_matches`** ([#229](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/229)). `resolveCik` returned only the first 5 of a name's candidates ("American" has 66, American Airlines 9th), so the company meant could be missing from the list. A partial list ends `Showing 10 of 66 matches; …`; a list of 10 or fewer has no such sentence. `secedgar_search_filings`' `ticker:` token ends `retry with its ticker or CIK.`
- **`secedgar_get_institutional_holdings` entity-search ambiguity reports SEC's own count** ([#230](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/230)): `data.total_matches` and the `Showing 10 of 598 matches; …` sentence. When SEC reports a floor (`capital`: 10,000, `gte`), the sentence reads `of at least 10,000` and `data.total_matches_is_lower_bound` is `true`.
- **`secedgar_compare_companies` `no_companies_resolved` message repeats each input's failure message** ([#231](https://github.com/cyanheads/secedgar-mcp-server/issues/231)), candidates included, so `content[]` names what `data.failed_companies` does.
